Weaving Data

Book design, web design, poster design, interactive workshop

Inspired by Giorgia Lupi and Anni Albers, this project introduces new ways of visualizing our personal data through weaving, which has historically been connected to passing down stories and information. It is also connected to the jacquard loom, of which the early computer punch cards were based on. Examples include incorporating your date of birth, area of origin, and MBTI into the mini tapestry.
